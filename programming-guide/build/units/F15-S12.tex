\subsection*{Bounded Types}

Bounded types consists of \textbf{Bounded Scalar} and \textbf{Bounded ITuple}. Bounded Scalar takes a range of [0, ub), where 'ub' represents its upper bound. Therefore, if an integer 'p' is set as bounded, it should also be associated with a specific upper bound. To establish such associations, programmers must code explicitly inside the \emph{Control Structures} of 'parallel-by' and 'with-in', which will be introduced later.

Similarly, since \emph{ITuple} is a group of \emph{Integer}s, it can also be associated with a group of bounds. Specifically, in Croqtile, the \emph{Bounded ITuple} is associated with a \emph{mdspan} value, where a group of upper bounds are settled. In later sections, you shall illustrate the detailed syntax.
