\subsection*{Look-Ahead: Bounded I-Tuples}

\emph{I-tuples} are not frequently used in real Croqtile code. However, their variants, \textbf{bounded i-tuple}s, which bind an \emph{i-tuple} with an \emph{mdspan} to indicate its possible range, are essential for constructing Croqtile loops. You will cover \emph{bounded i-tuples} in more detail in later chapters.
