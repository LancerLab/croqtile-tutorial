\subsection*{期值与循环简写}

\begin{table}[htbp]\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{语法} & \textbf{含义} \\
\midrule
\texttt{dma.copy.async src =\textgreater{} dst} & 非阻塞拷贝，立即返回 \\
\texttt{dma.any} & 占位期值，尚无传输在途 \\
\texttt{swap(f0, f1)} & 交换两个期值句柄，不拷贝数据 \\
\texttt{rotate(f0, f1, f2)} & 轮转三个期值句柄 \\
\texttt{with tile\_k in N ...} & 绑定到范围为 N 的分块轴，作用域受限 \\
\texttt{foreach tile\_k(1:)} & 从索引 1 开始迭代 \\
\texttt{\_\_co\_\_ auto fn(...)} & 由 \texttt{return} 语句推导返回类型 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
